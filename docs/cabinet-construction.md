# MFC Flat-Pack Cabinet Reference

Scope: DIY custom cabinets from MFC, panels cut + edged + CNC-drilled to order by a Polish supplier
(centrum.meble.pl). Bias: simple, cheap, popular. Default joinery = confirmat screws. All dimensions in
mm. Numbers marked **(supplier)** follow meble.pl's published technical conventions and win over generic
norms where they differ; **(varies)** marks where the industry genuinely differs by brand/region.

> ⚠️ The supplier-specific drill numbers below come from web research of meble.pl's technical page.
> Verify the order-critical ones (confirmat hole Ø, minifix geometry) against the live editor before
> your first real order.

## 1. MFC / melamine-faced chipboard

MFC = particleboard (chipboard) core with melamine-impregnated decor paper thermally fused to both
faces. Cheap, flat, dimensionally stable, pre-finished. Core is NOT moisture resistant (use
P3/moisture-resistant board for wet areas).

Standard thicknesses (Europe/Poland):
- **18 mm** — default carcass + shelves + fronts **(supplier carcass = 18 mm)**. 16 mm is the other
  common carcass thickness **(varies: 16 vs 18 by brand/region)**.
- **18 / 25 / 28 / 38 mm** — worktops, thick fronts.
- **10, 8 mm** — light/decorative panels, drawer parts.
- **3 mm HDF** — cabinet backs and drawer bottoms, melamine-faced **(supplier back = 3 mm HDF)**.

Sheet sizes: nominal **2800 × 2070 mm** (Egger, Kronospan) dominant; also 2800 × 2100, 2620 × 2070. You
order cut pieces ("formatki") so sheet size only matters for the supplier's yield/cost, not design.

Edge banding is required because the cut chipboard core is exposed, porous, and swells with moisture.
Only the two faces come laminated; all four cut edges are raw and must be banded on any visible edge
(see §8).

Decor/texture vocabulary: *decor* = printed pattern (e.g. solid white, oak, concrete); *texture/finish*
= surface emboss (PM smooth, ST structured, matt, gloss, "synchronised pore"). Match decor code AND
texture when ordering matching edge band.

## 2. Carcass construction

A basic box ("korpus") = 2 sides + top + bottom + back, plus shelves and (base units) a plinth.

Panel names:
- **Side / gable ("bok")** — vertical full-height side panels; the reference for height and depth.
- **Top & bottom ("wieniec górny/dolny")** — horizontal panels.
- **Back ("plecy" / HDF)** — 3 mm HDF closing the rear; gives squareness/racking resistance.
- **Shelf ("półka")** — fixed or adjustable.
- **Plinth / toe-kick ("cokół")** — recessed base front + supports, or 4 adjustable legs + clip-on
  plinth board (the cheap/popular kitchen approach).
- **Stretchers / rails ("trawersy")** — base cabinets with no full top use two horizontal rails
  (front + back) tying the sides together and carrying the worktop. Drawer/sink units use rails.

Standard layout = **top/bottom BETWEEN the sides** (sides run full height — "long sides cover"). This is
the frameless / 32 mm convention and the supplier default. Therefore:
- Overall **width** W = side_t + internal + side_t. With 18 mm sides: **`top/bottom length = W − 36`**.
- Overall **height** H = side panel height (sides are full height).
- Carcass **depth** D = side panel depth; the back is recessed so the HDF back is smaller than D.
- Worktop overhangs the carcass at the front (~20–40 mm) and is not part of the box.

## 3. Joinery / fasteners — exact drilling

### Confirmat screw — DEFAULT cheap method
- Screw: generic **Ø7 × 50 mm**; **supplier machines for l = 45 mm confirmats**. Self-tapping in
  chipboard, no extra hardware, head visible on the outside (cap if exposed).
- Drilling:
  - Through the **face** of the joining panel: **Ø8 mm** clearance hole, countersunk for the head.
  - Into the **edge** (end-grain) of the mating panel: **Ø4 mm** pilot, depth ≈ 35–40 mm. (Generic
    guides cite Ø5, but the meble.pl editor's edge drilling offers only Ø4/Ø8 and **Ø4 works fine for a
    standard confirmat** — use Ø4.)
  - Edge hole **centered ~8–9 mm** in an 18 mm panel; first/last screw **≥ 50 mm from the panel end**.
- Use: primary carcass assembly.

### Minifix / cam lock (eccentric, "mimośród")
Knock-down, invisible from outside.
- Cam housing **Ø15 mm** bored into the **face**, depth ~12.5–13.5 mm **(supplier = Ø15)**.
- Connecting bolt + dowel pressed into the mating panel **edge**.
- **(supplier)** Minifix 15, pin "**34 + 11 mm**": in the rail/shelf — **Ø15** cam + **Ø8** dowel hole;
  in the side panel edge — **Ø5** bolt hole.
- Generic Häfele: cam center distance B = 24 mm (Ø8 bolt) or 34 mm (Ø10 bolt) from the front edge; cam
  normally on the 32 mm line, 37 mm from front.

### Rafix (cam-style, faster pop-in)
- **Rafix 20**: housing **Ø20 mm** bore, depth ~14 mm; connecting bolt for **Ø5 mm** hole; works in
  ≥16 mm board. Pricier than Minifix; for cheap+popular prefer confirmat or Minifix.

### Wooden dowels (Ø8 mm)
Alignment + glue, paired with cams/confirmats (rarely structural alone in chipboard).
- **(supplier)** Ø8 mm, **l = 30 mm** (l = 50 mm for corner cabinets). Hole **Ø8**, ~15 mm deep each
  side; center on the edge centerline (~9 mm in 18 mm board).

## 4. Concealed hinges (euro, 35 mm cup, clip-on)  — needed once FRONTS are added
- **Cup bore Ø35 mm** in the door back, depth **~11.5–13 mm** **(varies)**.
- **Boring distance** (door edge to cup edge): **3–6 mm**, commonly ~5 mm. Cup center to door edge
  **≈ 22.5 mm**. Larger boring distance = more overlay.
- Two cup-fixing screws Ø ~2.5–3.5 mm, spacing **45 mm** (32 mm system) / 48 mm (older). Supplier drills
  **Ø35 + 2 screw points**.
- Cup center from top/bottom of door: ~80–100 mm **(varies)**; 2 hinges, +1 over ~900–1000 mm, +1 over
  ~1600 mm.
- **Mounting plate** on the cabinet side: two screws on the **32 mm line, 37 mm from the front edge**.
- Overlay: **full** (one door per side, crank ~0), **half** (two doors share a side, crank ~9.5), **inset**
  (flush, crank ~16–17). Most flat-pack = full overlay.

## 5. Drawer slides — needed once drawers are added
- **Roller slides ("rolkowe")** — cheapest, ~¾ extension, ~25 kg, side clearance ~12.5 mm/side.
- **Ball-bearing full-extension ("kulkowe")** — popular, full extension, soft-close common.
  - **Side clearance = 12.7 mm (½") per side** → **drawer box width = cabinet internal − 25.4 mm**
    (use 26 to be safe). The single most load-bearing number for drawer sizing.
- Lengths (50 mm steps): **250–550** (up to 700). Slide length ≤ carcass internal depth.
- Mounting: cabinet member on the **32 mm line, front holes 37 mm from the front edge**.

## 6. The 32 mm system (System 32)
One drilling grid so hinges, plates, slides, shelf pins interchange.
- **Vertical pitch = 32 mm** between centers (two parallel rows per side).
- **Hole Ø = 5 mm** (system + shelf-pin holes). Deeper bores (Ø8/15/35) sit on the same grid.
- **Front-row setback = 37 mm** from the front edge of the side panel. Rear row = front + N×32 mm.
- Keep all fittings on the 37 mm line and 32 mm pitch — the supplier's CNC expects this.

## 7. Back panel (3 mm HDF)
1. **Surface-mounted** (nailed/stapled/screwed to the rear edges) — cheapest, simplest; back = full
   carcass W × H; sits proud at the back. Common budget default. **(supplier default)**
2. **Rebate ("wpust/felc")** — rebate milled in the rear inside edge; back drops in flush.
3. **Groove ("rowek/nut")** — groove in all four panels; back fully captured (can't disassemble after).
   Typical groove: width ~4 mm, depth ~8–10 mm, setback ~10–15 mm **(varies)**. Grooved back size =
   internal + 2×groove_depth on each axis.

## 8. Edge banding
- Material: **ABS** (durable, no PVC — current standard) or melamine (cheapest, brittle). Match decor +
  texture code to the board.
- Thicknesses: **0.4 / 0.5 / 0.8 / 1.0 / 2.0 mm**. **(supplier)** ~0.8 mm carcass / **2.0 mm fronts**.
  The meble.pl editor offers **1 mm and 2 mm** variants.
- Band **every visible/exposed edge**; hidden edges (against a wall, buried in a groove) can be raw.
- **Covering long vs short** ("kryjące długie / krótkie"): the band on the continuous/long-visible edge
  runs through, and the abutting (short) edge butts into it, hiding the seam on the less-seen face.
  E.g. on a side panel the **front edge band runs full height** and the top/bottom bands butt against
  it. Default = **długie (long covers)**.

## 9. Standard dimensions (Europe / IKEA-style)

Kitchen base: carcass height **~720** (710–720); legs/plinth **100–150** + worktop 20–40 → worktop
height **≈ 850–910**; plinth/toe-kick **100–150**; depth **560–580**; widths **150/200/300/400/450/500/
600/800/900/1000/1200** (popular 300/400/500/600/800/900).

Wall (upper): depth **300–350**; heights **360/575–600/720/900**; mounted 450–600 above worktop.

Tall/pantry: **2000–2400** tall, depth 560–600.

Wardrobes: depth **580–600** (600 standard so hangers clear); shelf depth ~580; rail centerline ~300–320
from back; long-hang clear 1600–1800; double-hang 2 × 900–1000; sliding doors need ~700+ depth.
